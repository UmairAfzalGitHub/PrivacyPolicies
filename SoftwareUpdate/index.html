2. Information Collection & Use

We classify the information processed by our App into two main categories: non-personal technical data collected automatically, and permissions accessed locally to perform core functionality.

A. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

To provide update detection, application management, storage optimization, and phone hardware diagnostics, the App evaluates system data on-device. This data remains on your physical hardware and is not uploaded to our infrastructure.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Information

To support advertising, perform error reporting, and analyze overall stability, third-party analytics and ad network services integrated into the App may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information, including:

  • Device information (e.g., device model, manufacturer, operating system version, system language, screen resolution).
  • Network & connectivity status (e.g., IP address, general country/city level location derived from IP).
  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Android Advertising ID / GAID).
  • App usage & performance metrics (e.g., launch frequency, feature interaction, session duration, crash logs, diagnostic traces).

C. Information Provided Voluntarily

If you contact us directly for customer support or feedback via email, we will receive your name, email address, and the contents of your inquiry. We use this information strictly to resolve your request and improve our App.
